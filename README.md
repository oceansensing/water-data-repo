# water-data-repo

The world's lakes and the Americas' rivers, as vector tiles for the
oceansensing.org ocean map: HydroLAKES's lakes and reservoirs of 10 ha and
more, with their names, type, area, mean depth, volume and surface elevation,
and HydroSHEDS v2's streams of North and South America draining 1 km² and
more, with their Strahler order and upstream area.

Checked daily.
